<p>A Matrix chat client in the spirit of Element X, built on the Matrix Rust SDK.</p>
<ul>
<li>Works with matrix.org, public servers or your own homeserver</li>
<li>Sign in with OAuth, password or single sign-on</li>
<li>End-to-end encryption with device verification and key backup</li>
<li>Replies, threads, reactions, edits, polls, voice messages and files</li>
<li>Search inside chats, including encrypted ones</li>
<li>Push notifications through ntfy, with a background check as a fallback</li>
<li>Link previews, drafts and six chat bubble styles</li>
<li>Create rooms, direct chats, explore public rooms, moderation</li>
<li>Device list and app lock with PIN and fingerprint</li>
<li>Two-pane layout on tablets and foldables</li>
</ul>
<p>Needs a server with Simplified Sliding Sync (Synapse 1.114 or newer). 64-bit ARM only.</p>
<p>Google-free and de-Googled: no Google Play services, Firebase or other Google services, and the app never contacts Google. It works the same on de-Googled phones (GrapheneOS, CalyxOS, /e/OS, LineageOS without Google apps).</p>
<p>Telemetry-free: no analytics, crash reporting, usage stats or tracking of any kind.</p>
